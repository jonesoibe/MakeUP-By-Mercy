Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a makeup session take?

A typical makeup session takes 45-60 minutes depending on the style. Bridal makeup takes slightly longer (60-90 minutes) to ensure perfection. We'll confirm the exact timing when you book.

Do you offer travel services?

Yes! We offer travel services for events and occasions. Please mention your location when booking, and we'll confirm availability and any travel fees.

What products do you use?

We use professional-grade, high-quality makeup products that are long-lasting, cruelty-free, and hypoallergenic. All products are suitable for all skin types.

Can I request a specific makeup style?

Absolutely! We love custom requests. Feel free to share photos, inspiration, or describe your ideal look when you book. We'll work with you to create the perfect makeup.

What's your cancellation policy?

We require 24 hours notice for cancellations. If you need to reschedule, contact us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to accommodate your new date.

Do you offer makeup lessons?

Yes! We offer personalized makeup lessons where we teach you our techniques. Perfect for learning professional makeup application skills. Contact us for pricing.
